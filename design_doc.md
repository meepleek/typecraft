## Combat

### Types

- charge attack (e.g. axe swing)
    - type word(s) to charge the weapon
        - more powerful attacks need more typing
    - stab -> press specific char for each enemy to hit them
    - swing -> press a directional button to swing an arc (& move?) in the given dir
- ranged (spear/bow) -> attack at range
    - have to pick weapon/ammo back up
- explosives
    - big area, mines
    - hurts the player too

### Patterns (weapons and/or explosives)

- ortho
- diag
- range upto 2
- range exactly 2
- chained - type multiple enemies, then activate
    - could behave a bit like range without having to pick ammo back up?
